Our Story

Built with passion, precision, and a shared commitment to beautiful spaces.

Ashley & Travis — A Couple-Owned Tile Startup

We’re Ashley and Travis, a couple who turned our shared love for design, hands-on work, and problem-solving into a tile installation business built from the ground up in North Carolina. What started as small home projects quickly grew into a full-time craft — one fueled by careful planning, clean execution, and a genuine passion for transforming bathrooms into beautiful, functional spaces.

Every project we take on reflects our belief that great tile work is more than just installation — it’s an art form. We approach each job with patience, precision, and a commitment to getting every detail right, from layout and leveling to grout lines and finishing touches. Our focus on bathrooms allows us to specialize deeply, delivering upgrades that feel intentional, cohesive, and built to last.
